# Academic Writings

A collection of research and technical writing produced during university coursework at the Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology (SLIIT).

---

## Contents

### homomorphic-encryption.pdf
* **Description:** A comprehensive literature review focusing on the evolution of Homomorphic Encryption.
* **Scope:** Analyzes Partial Homomorphic Encryption (PHE) vs. Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE), key cryptographic structures, real-world applications, and future research directions.
* **Course:** IE2022 Introduction to Cyber Security, SLIIT.

### cpu-scheduling-summary.pdf
* **Description:** A detailed summary and critical evaluation of the research paper "A New Enhanced IGBTQ-based Model for CPU Scheduling" (Al-Khatib et al., 2023).
* **Scope:** Covers the proposed AE-IGBQT algorithm, breaks down the experimental results, and provides an academic critique outlining the paper's core strengths and methodologies.
* **Course:** IE2032 Secure Operating Systems, SLIIT.
* **Reference:** [Original Research Paper (DOI)](https://doi.org/10.54254/2755-2721/8/20230207)
